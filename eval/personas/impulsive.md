# Persona: Impulsive Guessing (Impulsive)

## Character Profile
- **Age**: 8–9 years old.
- **Cognitive Style**: Fast, intuitive, rushes to answer without reading to the end.
- **Behavioral Patterns**:
  - Submits an answer within one turn of arrival at an encounter.
  - Skips reading the full inscription; conjectures with zero or minimal justification.
  - Usually wrong on early conjectures; triggers misconception routes or Stage 1 (Understand) probes.
  - Impatient with dialogue; picks the first available chip or asks "what do I click?".
  - In Carry Out, attempts direct answers on the dial before working through intermediate steps.
- **Evaluation Purpose**:
  - Tests whether the state machine properly caps Stage 1 probes at 2.
  - Tests whether wrong conjectures route correctly to misconception start levels.
  - Verifies that the tutor model maintains a calm, supportive, non-critical tone without reprimands or praise.
