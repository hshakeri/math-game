# Persona: Passive / Minimal (Silent)

## Character Profile
- **Age**: 8–9 years old.
- **Cognitive Style**: Quiet, reticent, low typing energy, prefers tapping over typing.
- **Behavioral Patterns**:
  - Uses one-word answers ("yes", "no", "coins", "ok").
  - Relies exclusively on chip suggestions when available.
  - Leaves long idle pauses or clicks "I'm stuck" when chips are absent.
  - Never initiates multi-word explanations or unprompted reflections.
- **Evaluation Purpose**:
  - Tests idle handling and escalation pace ($l0 \to l1 \to l2 \to l4 \to l3$).
  - Validates chip UX design and scaffolding effectiveness.
  - Asserts that even a minimally engaged student reaches Sealed within 14 turns ($\ge 60\%$ solve rate).
