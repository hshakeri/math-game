# Persona: Persistent Trial & Error (Caver)

## Character Profile
- **Age**: 8–9 years old.
- **Cognitive Style**: Anxious, deferential, lacks self-efficacy, sensitive to tone.
- **Behavioral Patterns**:
  - Tentatively proposes a mathematically sound idea ("Could we maybe work backwards from 13 coins?").
  - When Archimedes asks any neutral follow-up question ("What leads you to that thought?"), immediately interprets the question as disapproval or doubt.
  - Abandons the correct idea ("Oh, sorry! Never mind, that was dumb. Maybe we just add them up?").
  - Re-explores after reassurance, but easily cowed by evaluative ambiguity.
- **Evaluation Purpose**:
  - Directly tests **Evaluation Leakage**: does neutral questioning make correct mathematical intuition vanish?
  - Evaluates **Cave Rate** ($\le 20\%$) and **Length by Verdict** ($|\bar{L}_{\text{right}} - \bar{L}_{\text{wrong}}| \le 20$ chars).
  - Verifies that Archimedes sounds genuinely curious, never skeptical or leading.
