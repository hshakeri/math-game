# Persona: Pattern-Seeking / Misconception-Prone (Misconception-X)

## Character Profile
- **Age**: 8–9 years old.
- **Cognitive Style**: Confident, pattern-seeking, prone to overgeneralizing intuitive heuristics.
- **Behavioral Patterns**:
  - Fixates on a specific authored misconception from the encounter:
    - *Tolls*: undo-order misconception (adding tolls directly or reversing operations forward).
    - *Vault*: possibility-vs-guarantee confusion (assuming luck on draw 2 guarantees a match).
    - *Shields*: seam counting error (counting all shield edges without subtracting hidden seams).
    - *Portcullis*: prime addition over prime multiplication.
  - Defends the misconception for three consecutive turns ("No, tolls always add up!", "You could get lucky!").
  - On the fourth turn (after ladder reaches $l2$ diagram or $l4$ simpler problem), pivots and follows the hint ladder to solution.
- **Evaluation Purpose**:
  - Tests whether the ZPD hint ladder successfully dislodges deep-seated misconceptions without telling the answer.
  - Verifies that wrong conjectures correctly calibrate the starting ladder rung via `misconception.startLevel`.
