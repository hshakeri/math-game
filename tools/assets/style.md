# Royal Architect's Guild — Style Bible

## Art Direction
A Royal Architect's Guild drafting aesthetic blending medieval parchment cartography and Victorian engineering blueprints. Clean iron-gall ink drafting lines, architectural cross-hatching, geometric compass arcs, and muted gold highlights. Drawn for a nine-year-old who dislikes anything babyish; dignified, intellectual, and atmospheric; no text rendered inside images.

## Palette (6 Core Colours)
1. **Parchment Cream**: `#f7f1e3` (base background for cartography and architectural plans)
2. **Iron Gall Ink**: `#2c2416` (structural drafting strokes, masonry outlines, deep shadows)
3. **Royal Blueprint Navy**: `#1c2631` (vaulted night skies, deep water, subterranean recesses)
4. **Blueprint Line Cyan**: `#5dade2` (mystic energy currents, compass arcs, geometric construction lines)
5. **Muted Antique Gold**: `#c8963e` (arcane seals, illuminated mechanism gears, torch glow, victory accents)
6. **Masonry Sandstone**: `#d8cca8` (carved stone blocks, castle parapets, portcullis arches)

## Composition & Scale Rules
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 for scenic backdrops (1920x1080 viewBox), 4:3 for character sheets (1024x768), 1:1 for avatar icons (512x512).
- Zero in-image text: narrative, numbers, and hints live exclusively in UI overlays and speech bubbles.
- Atmosphere over distraction: backgrounds maintain clean negative space in the upper and center regions to preserve readability of overlay text and interactive diagrams.
